HOW IT WORKS
i
Make the sheet
One tab per deck. Column A is the front of the card, column B is the back. Name the tabs the way you want the decks named.
ii
Share it by link
In Google Sheets, choose Share, then Anyone with the link, as a viewer. No sign-in is ever asked for on the watch.
iii
Send the link to the watch
Make the Zip Flip link below and send it in Messages. Tapped on the watch, it opens the app and every tab shows up as a deck.
iv
Flip
Read the front, tap, check the back. Got it or Again. Cards you know come back later and later; cards you miss come back tomorrow.
MAKE THE LINK

Paste the address of your sheet.

Send in Messages

Send it to the watch in Messages and tap it there. Whoever can open the sheet can open the link, so share it the way you would share the sheet. Send in Messages works on an iPhone.
